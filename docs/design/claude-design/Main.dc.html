Design system

Safe payments for social sellers. Plain, calm and reassuring — in English and Amharic, light and dark, from a 360 px phone to a 1100 px desktop.

Words before colour

Every status is written out in a badge. Colour only supports the label, never replaces it.

Phone first

Designed at 360 px with 44 px targets and plain forms that submit without JavaScript.
